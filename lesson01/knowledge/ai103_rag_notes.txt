Microsoft Foundry is a platform for building, evaluating, and deploying generative AI applications and agents.

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) retrieves relevant information from a knowledge source and supplies that information to a language model to help ground its response.

Chunking splits a document into smaller passages. Chunk size affects the amount of context available for retrieval, while overlap helps preserve context across chunk boundaries.

Keyword search matches query terms against document text. Vector search compares numerical representations called embeddings to find semantically similar content. Hybrid search combines keyword and vector retrieval.

Groundedness measures whether an answer's claims are supported by the provided evidence.

Answer relevance measures whether the answer addresses the user's question.

Recall at k measures how many of the relevant items are retrieved among the top k results. Precision at k measures what proportion of the top k results are relevant.
